# Mink vs Sherpa vs Fleece vs Microfibre Blanket: Which Is Warmest for Indian Winters?

**Short answer:** for the coldest North Indian nights, a **heavy mink blanket (about 2-2.5 kg)** is the warmest single layer of the four. A **sherpa-backed blanket** comes close at a lower weight. **Polar fleece** is the light, cheap option for mild winters and AC rooms. "**Microfibre**" is not really a separate category: most mink, sherpa and fleece blankets sold in India are polyester microfibre underneath. The label tells you the *finish*. The **weight and GSM** tell you the warmth.

This guide explains what each name actually means, how to pick by city and room, and links to blankets that are live on our deals pages right now.

## The quick answer

- **Coldest nights (Delhi, Punjab, UP, hills):** heavy mink, 2-2.5 kg for a double bed.
- **Cold nights, but you hate a heavy blanket:** a 400 GSM sherpa-backed blanket.
- **Mild winters and AC rooms (Mumbai, Bengaluru, Chennai):** polar fleece.
- **Plain "microfibre" flannel:** an AC blanket or a second layer, not a winter blanket on its own.
- **Ignore the label, read the weight.** Warmth tracks kg and GSM, not the word "mink".

## What the labels really mean

The names on Amazon and Flipkart are finishes, not fibres. Almost every blanket in this price band is polyester.

- **Mink** is a dense, short, velvety polyester pile. It is named for the feel, not the animal. In India it is usually sold by **weight**: a 1 kg mink is an AC-room blanket, and a 2-2.5 kg mink is a proper winter blanket.
- **Sherpa** is a curly, fluffy pile that imitates sheep's wool. It is usually bonded to a smoother face, so the blanket has two sides: a thick, fleecy side and a flat flannel or micromink side.
- **Fleece (polar fleece)** is a brushed, flat polyester knit. It is light, quick-drying and inexpensive, but it is thin.
- **Microfibre** is the fibre itself: very fine polyester strands. "Microfibre blanket" on a listing can mean any of the above, or a thin flannel throw. Read the weight, not the word.

## How a blanket keeps you warm

A blanket does not make heat. It traps still air near your body. Three things decide how much air it holds:

1. **Pile depth (loft).** Taller, curlier fibres hold more air. This is why a sherpa face feels warmer than flat fleece of similar weight.
2. **Density and weight.** More fibre per square metre means fewer gaps for heat to escape. This is why a 2.5 kg mink beats a 1 kg mink of the same size.
3. **Draught-proofing.** A heavy blanket drapes over your shoulders and seals the edges. A light one lifts at the sides and lets cold air in.

## GSM and weight: the numbers that matter

GSM (grams per square metre) measures fabric density. Weight in kg is GSM multiplied by area, so the same GSM feels heavier on a king-size blanket. As a practical rule of thumb for Indian winters:

- **Under ~250 GSM / under 1 kg:** AC rooms and very mild winters.
- **~280-400 GSM / 1.2-2 kg:** cool nights, or one layer in a mild city.
- **400+ GSM / 2-2.5 kg+:** cold North Indian nights as a single layer.

## Care tips so it stays warm

- **Wash cold on a gentle cycle** and skip fabric softener. Softener coats the fibres and flattens the pile, which reduces trapped air.
- **Dry flat or on a line in the sun.** High dryer heat can melt polyester tips and leave sherpa matted.
- **Brush a matted sherpa side** gently with a soft brush once it is dry.
- **Wash heavy mink only when needed.** A 2.5 kg blanket holds a lot of water and can take a day or two to dry in winter sun. Air it out weekly instead.

## FAQ

**Is mink or sherpa warmer?**
At similar weights, sherpa's curly pile traps a little more air. In practice, the heavy 2-2.5 kg mink blankets sold in India are warmer than most sherpa blankets, because they carry much more fabric.

**Is a mink blanket made from real mink?**
No. Mink blankets sold in India are polyester. "Mink" describes the soft, dense pile.

**Is fleece enough for Delhi winters?**
Not on its own for December and January nights. Use it as a top layer over a quilt, or choose a heavy mink or 400+ GSM sherpa.

**Which blanket is best for humid cities like Mumbai or Chennai?**
A light polar fleece or a cotton dohar. Heavy polyester mink traps heat and moisture and can feel clammy.

**Does a higher price mean a warmer blanket?**
Not reliably. Compare the weight in kg and the GSM. A ₹999 2.5 kg mink is often warmer than a ₹2,000 light plush throw.
