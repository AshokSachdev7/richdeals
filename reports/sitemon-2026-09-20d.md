### Re-grounding #1 — the `/` 2.0 s flag is withdrawn

Last tick flagged `/` at 2.016 s as "the slowest thing on the site". This tick it returned in
**0.372 s at the identical 314,785-byte payload**. Same bytes, 5.4x faster: that is a **cold ISR
render**, not a standing performance defect. The page revalidates, the first request after expiry
eats the render, every request after it is served from cache.

**Not carried forward as rot.** A flag that only appears when you happen to hit the revalidation
window is noise, and repeating it every tick would train the audit to ignore real regressions.

**Zero rows have an mrp present.** A null `discountPct` where `mrp` is null is correct arithmetic —
there is nothing to compute a discount from. So last tick's arm reading **0 was right**, and the

not a safe silent fix inside a monitoring tick.
