## Method

- **Sweep:** `/deals?page=N` plus `/deals/superdeals`, ≥2.5 s between requests. The base64 `?rto=` Buy Now links were resolved to store URLs.
- **Stage 1:** 93 rows: 51 fresh and 42 already in the DB.
- **Amazon check:** in-tab `fetch('/dp/ASIN?th=1&psc=1')` in the logged-in tab:
  - price from `#corePriceDisplay_desktop_feature_div .a-price-whole`
  - MRP from `.basisPrice`
  - stock from `#availability` and `#add-to-cart-button`
- **Flipkart check:** in-tab `fetch('/x/p/x?pid=PID')` in the Flipkart tab, reading the ld+json `offers` price and availability.
  - The product page for the diya holder had no `/p/itm…` path for its own pid. A Flipkart `/search?q=` fetch returned the path anchored to that pid.
  - Link built as `/<slug>/p/itm…?pid=…&affid=djhackraj`.
- **MRP guard:** `(!m||m<=p||m>=p*100||p/m<=0.2) → null`. Where it fires, the title and description carry no % or M.R.P. text.
- **Push:** `apps/api/_ifs24j-push.cjs` (18 Amazon) and `apps/api/_ifs24j-push2.cjs` (1 Flipkart). The ₹, % and duplicate-pid validators all passed.
- **Re-price:** `apps/api/_ifs24j-upd.cjs` via `prisma.deal.update`. Unverified copy was removed from the descriptions before saving: a stock count, "peels off clean", and the Havells colour-mode and hub claims.
