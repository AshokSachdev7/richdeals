## Method

- **Sweep:** `/deals?page=N` plus `/deals/superdeals`, at least 2.5 s between IFS requests.
- **Resolve:** each base64 `?rto=` Buy Now was resolved to the real store URL.
- **Dedup:** every `productId` was checked against the live DB. The 13 pushed IDs were re-checked just before the POST (0 hits).
- **Amazon check:** in-page `fetch('/dp/<ASIN>?th=1&psc=1')` in the logged-in Playwright tab, reading:
  - Price: `#centerCol .priceToPay`.
  - MRP: `.basisPrice`.
  - Stock: `#availability` plus `#add-to-cart-button`.
- **Flipkart check:** in-page `fetch('/x/p/x?pid=<PID>')`, reading ld+json `Product.offers`.
- **Price rule:** a deal is rejected if the page price is more than ₹1 above the card price.
