## Method
- Ran `ingest-ifs-proper.mjs` stage 1 over `/deals` and `/deals/superdeals`. Base64 `?rto=` Buy Now ids were resolved to merchant URLs.
- Amazon PDPs were read with desktop curl (`#corePriceDisplay .a-price-whole`, `#availability`, add-to-cart) because the Playwright MCP is hung. The Myntra PDP was read the same way.
- Flipkart has no curl path (403 reCAPTCHA), so all Flipkart candidates were dropped.
- Dedup was by productId against the live DB. New rows were created with Prisma `deal.create` plus a `priceHistory` row. Dups were updated with `deal.update` plus `priceHistory`, never the bulk upsert, so no slug was rewritten.
- Text was rewritten in our own words. Images come from `m.media-amazon.com` / `assets.myntassets.com`.
