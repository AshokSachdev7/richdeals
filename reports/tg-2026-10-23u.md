## Method

- **Read:** one `browser_evaluate` over the Telegram Web sidebar (Playwright, profile richDeals) for the 13 groups in `data/tg-groups.json`, plus a reload-and-scrape of the busier chats.
- **Resolve:** each shortlink (`link.amazon`, `amzn.to`, `amazn.lt`, `fkrt.co`, `fkrt.it`, `fkrt.pe`) was resolved to the real product URL before dedup.
- **Dedup:** against `data/tg-multi-seen.json` (array) and the live DB `productId`. Every dedup hit on a LIVE row was re-read on its product page. The 8 product IDs were re-checked against the DB just before the push, with 0 hits.
- **Amazon check:** done in the logged-in tab with an in-page `fetch('/dp/<ASIN>?th=1&psc=1')`:
  - Price: `#centerCol .priceToPay`.
  - MRP: `.basisPrice`.
  - Stock: `#availability` plus `#add-to-cart-button`.
  - Image: `#landingImage[data-old-hires]`.
- **Flipkart check:** in-tab fetch of the product page, then the ld+json Product for price, availability and image. The MRP comes from the page's `"mrp"` value.
- **Price rule:** a deal is rejected if the page price is more than ₹1 above the posted price.
- **MRP check:** if price/MRP ≤ 0.2, the MRP is nulled and the copy carries no % or M.R.P.
