## PRIORITY: stale Offer prices
`[dealSlug]/page.tsx` omits `priceValidUntil` when `dealPriceCheck(deal).stale` is true. The check uses `apps/web/src/lib/site.ts:265`: the newest `PriceHistory.postedAt`, falling back to `createdAt`, and the price counts as stale once that date is more than 14 days old.

The page still shows the price, so the schema is not invalid. But Google sees an Offer with no validity window on a price that nobody has re-checked for months. That matches the earlier GSC finding that a stale Offer price leads to demotion.

None of these ASINs were in the ifs 10-29c PDP set, so none were incidentally re-verified.

**Site-wide:** 12,444 of 14,853 LIVE priced deals (84%) have a last check older than 14 days. No earlier schema report flagged this, so it is a new finding.

**Recommended fix (not applied, report only):** a price-resync pass, top-100 indexable and GSC pages first. For each deal, re-read the PDP (Amazon via `.a-price-whole` in `#corePriceDisplay` in the logged-in tab; other stores via ld+json) and write a `PriceHistory` row. That restores `priceValidUntil` and corrects any drifted price. Do not fake freshness by bumping dates without a real PDP read.
