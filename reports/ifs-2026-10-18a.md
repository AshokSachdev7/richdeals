## Method

- **Buy Now:** each `?rto=` base64 deal ID was resolved to the real store URL in stage 1 (`ingest-ifs-proper.mjs`).
- **Price check:** every Amazon ID was fetched in the logged-in tab (`/dp/ID?th=1&psc=1`), reading:
  - `#centerCol` price-to-pay and M.R.P.;
  - `#availability` and the add-to-cart button;
  - the `#landingImage` hi-res image.
- **Copy and links:**
  - Titles and descriptions are rewritten (no source text).
  - Images come from the m.media-amazon.com CDN.
  - Links use `?tag=ashoksachdev-21`.
- **Slug incident (fixed):** a sed edit to the build script broke the slugify regex, so the 9 rows were created with mangled slugs. Example: `london-notes-ocean-tide-mini-perume-or-men-15-ml-…`.
  - The correct slugs were set by productId seconds later.
  - All 9 now return 200 from the API.
  - Nothing was pinged under the bad slugs.
