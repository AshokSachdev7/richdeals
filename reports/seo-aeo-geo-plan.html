Read the traffic number correctly

3 clicks / 164 impressions over 28 days means almost nothing on this site is ranked, not that nothing is indexed. 297 posts producing 164 impressions is a visibility problem at the query level: we hold pages for topics nobody searches, or we hold them at positions nobody scrolls to. Adding a 298th post of the same kind changes nothing. The gap export matters precisely because it names queries with demand attached.

04 · Strategy

Three layers on one page, not three programmes

SEO, AEO and GEO are not separate workstreams here. They are three things a single well-built page must do, and the page template already does two of them. The distinction only matters in what we verify.

SEO — be eligible and be relevant

  • Query-first titles. The gap export hands us the exact phrasing people use — recharge of airtel, jio balance check missed call number. The H1 and seoTitle answer that phrasing, not a prettier version of it.
  • One page per intent, never per keyword. how-to-check-jio-number and vi-number-check-code are different operators, so different pages. Four Netflix-plan slugs are one page.
  • Internal links carry the topic. Each cluster gets a hub and every spoke links up; the hub links down to its spokes. 297 orphan-ish posts is the current state.
  • IndexNow on every publish. Already enforced — publishing runs through insert-blog-mdmeta.mjs, which pings. A batch that skipped the ping is not shipped.

AEO — be the extractable answer

  • Answer in the first 60 words. The code, the number, the price — before any preamble. *333# appears in sentence one of the Jio post, not in section four.
  • HowTo + FAQPage already auto-fire. blog/[slug]/page.tsx parses a ## How to… heading with numbered steps into HowTo schema, and a ## FAQ heading into FAQPage. 264 of 297 posts already trip the FAQ path; 97 trip HowTo.
  • Visible copy must match the schema. Non-negotiable. The FAQ renders on the page; schema-only markup is a manual-action risk, not a shortcut.
  • Valid schema is not eligibility. We verify the JSON-LD parses and matches the visible page. We do not claim a rich result will appear.

GEO — be citable when quoted

  • Self-contained passages. An answer engine lifts one section, not the page. Each ## block must make sense with zero context above it — no “as mentioned above”.
  • Attributable facts, sourced. The port guide cites TRAI’s MNP rules; the balance guide cites Jio’s own help pages. Fact plus named source is what survives extraction.
  • Crawler access stays open. robots.ts already allows GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, ChatGPT-User, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot and Google-Extended. These are distinct controls for distinct jobs and stay distinct.
  • Measure honestly. One prompt returning a citation is a sample, not a report. Track it as a log, never as a ranking.

05 · Execution

The phases

Publishing is capped at 4 posts per IST day by hard rule and enforced in code. That cap, not research capacity, sets every date below.

Phase 0 — Template work

shipped 2026-09-13 done

HowTo and FAQPage extraction shipped in blog/[slug]/page.tsx and verified live in production on a new post: 1 Article, 1 BreadcrumbList, 1 FAQPage with 6 Question/Answer pairs, 1 HowTo with 5 HowToStep, plus Organization, WebSite and ImageObject. No per-post storage and no migration — every existing post that already has the right headings gained the schema on deploy.

Phase 1 — Telecom utility cluster

2026-09-13 → 09-26 · ~40 posts

The whole cluster, hub-and-spoke, at 3 posts a day. These queries are asked by people holding a phone with a problem, the answers are public and verifiable, and the difficulty band is the softest of any cluster with real volume.

  • Hub: /blog/how-to-save-on-mobile-recharge-india-2026 — already live, becomes the cluster index.
  • Every spoke links up to the hub and out to /offers; the hub gains a linked index of spokes as they ship.
  • Each post opens with the literal code or number, then a numbered ## How to… block, then ## FAQ.
  • Facts sourced from the operator’s own help pages or TRAI. No screenshots of tariffs, which change.

Phase 2 — Low-difficulty how-to

2026-09-27 → 10-08 · ~30 posts

The account-and-order how-tos: cancel a Flipkart order, delete PhonePe history, track a Myntra order, check a PhonePe balance. Same template, same schema path. These are the cheapest remaining wins in the export — several sit at SD 13–24.

  • Hub: a new /blog/shopping-account-help-india-2026 index, linking every spoke.
  • Each post links to the relevant store hub we already own, which gives the 253 hub pages internal links they currently lack.

Phase 3 — Store hubs, upgraded not created

2026-10-09 → 10-18 · no new posts

The store-hub cluster wants pages we already have. redbus, KFC, Zomato, Swiggy, Rapido, Flipkart, Adidas, Naaptol all exist as hubs. The work is template-level, so it lands on all 253 at once rather than one page at a time.

  • Per-store intro copy answering “how do coupon codes work at this store” — written, not generated from a pattern.
  • FAQPage on the hub template, built from real hub fields the way dealFaq() already does for deal pages.
  • Hubs link to their category, to related stores, and to the how-to posts from Phase 2.

Phase 4 — best-X, only where deals exist

2026-10-19 onward

468 rows, and most of them are not for us. The filter is one query against our own deal table: if we hold live, price-verified deals for the product category, the roundup has something real behind it and we build it. If we do not, we skip it — a roundup with no products is the scaled-content pattern we are refusing everywhere else.

  • Candidate leaders from the export: sunscreen for men, sulphate-free shampoo, facial cleanser for oily skin, body wash, pregnancy test kit, keypad phones.
  • Every product row carries a live price from our DB and a rel="sponsored nofollow" outbound through /out/:id.
  • Drop any roundup where fewer than 5 products have a current verified price. Better no page than a stale one.

Standing block on Phase 4 that the owner has to clear

Nothing currently refreshes prices on the 9,689 live deals. A best-X roundup is only as good as the prices in it, so Phase 4 is not safe to ship at scale until a price-refresh pass runs. The pass is written and waiting for the word. This is the largest unbuilt lever on the site and it blocks the largest cluster in the gap export.

07 · Link architecture

Hub and spoke, because 297 loose posts rank like 297 loose posts

Every post already carries at least one internal link by house rule. What is missing is direction — links that make a topic legible rather than just satisfying a count.

Telecom hub

  • Hub: how-to-save-on-mobile-recharge-india-2026
  • Every Phase 1 spoke links up to it in the closing section, in context, never as a bare “read more”.
  • Hub gains a linked spoke index, updated as each ships.
  • All spokes link to /offers where recharge cashback is live.

Payments & account hub

  • Hub: upi-cashback-offers-2026, with best-cashback-coupon-sites-india-2026 and cashback-vs-coupons-vs-reward-points-india as siblings.
  • PhonePe, Paytm and bank how-tos from Phase 2 attach here.

Store hubs

  • 253 hubs exist and are largely unlinked from editorial content.
  • Phase 2 fixes this as a side effect — each order/tracking how-to names its store and links the hub.
  • Hub template then links back to the how-to, closing the loop.

08 · Guardrails

What we will not do

Refused outright

  • Referral-code pages, the whole 32-row cluster.
  • State-wise liquor price pages.
  • Serial schedules, actor trivia, dumb-charades lists.
  • Any roundup whose products we cannot price-verify.
  • Bulk-deleting the 5 thin posts without reading them.

Claims we will not make

  • That a page will be indexed, ranked, or shown as a rich result.
  • That schema validity equals feature eligibility.
  • That any export estimate is our forecast traffic.
  • That one AI-engine citation is a measurement.
  • That a traffic change was caused by an update, from timing alone.

Rules already binding

  • 3–4 posts per IST day. Never 0, never 5 — enforced in the publish script.
  • Cover image and alt text on every post, no exceptions.
  • IndexNow ping on every publish, HTTP status reported.
  • Affiliate links rel="sponsored nofollow" through /out/:id.
  • Never copy source text or images.

The honest read on the ceiling

A prior audit found only 8 queries ranking at all, every one of them between position 26 and 68. That pattern — broad indexing, uniformly deep positions — points at domain authority rather than on-page quality, and no amount of schema fixes it. This plan is still the right work: it replaces low-intent pages with pages that have demand and a reason to be cited, which is the precondition for links. But if Phase 1 lands zero impressions by the decision date, the constraint is off-page, and the next tick should say so rather than writing post 340.

Limitations. No live SERP positions, backlink data or field CWV were pulled for this plan. Competitor gap figures are a single vendor’s export and were not cross-checked against a second source. Cluster verdicts are editorial judgment applied to those estimates, not measurements.
