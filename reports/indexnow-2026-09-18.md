### On the 422

Not URL-specific. After the 64-URL batch returned 200, *every* further POST in
the same minute returned 422 — including `/offers`, which had just been
accepted inside the 200 batch, and `/stores` + `/categories`, which had not been
submitted at all. api.indexnow.org is rate-limiting repeat POSTs from this key
within a short window, not rejecting the sitemap/feed content types. The Bing
GET endpoint has no such limit and took all three.

Practical rule: **one POST per tick, everything in it.** Anything that has to go
separately (sitemap, feed, llms.txt) goes straight to the Bing GET endpoint.
