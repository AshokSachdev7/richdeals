## Fix: Myntra links dropped as category pages

`NOT_A_PRODUCT` in `apps/api/scripts/lib/ingest-common.mjs` matches `/c/`. IFS links Myntra products as `/a/b/c/<styleId>/buy`, so every IFS Myntra deal was dropped before it reached the Myntra branch.

Myntra is now exempt from that generic check, because its own branch already enforces a numeric style id.
