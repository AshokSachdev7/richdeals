## Method

- **Sweep:** IFS `/deals?page=N` plus `/deals/superdeals`, with at least 2.5 s between requests. Each base64 `?rto=` Buy Now link was resolved to the merchant URL.
- **Amazon check:** in-tab `fetch('/dp/ASIN?th=1&psc=1')` in the logged-in tab:
  - price from `#corePriceDisplay_desktop_feature_div .priceToPay .a-price-whole`
  - stock from `#add-to-cart-button`
  - title from `#productTitle`
- **Flipkart check:** in-tab fetch of `/x/p/x?pid=PID`. A pid-anchored regex finds the `slug/p/itm…` path, and the ld+json `offers` gives the price.
- **Myntra check:** the shortlink HTML carries embedded JSON with `discountedPrice` and `mrp`.
- **MRP guard:** `(!m||m<=p||m>=p*100||p/m<=0.2) → null`. When it fires, the title and description carry no % or M.R.P. text.
- **Dedup:** every productId was checked against the live DB with Prisma (`_ifs24i-dedup.cjs`).
- **Push:** `apps/api/_ifs24i-push.cjs`. The ₹ and % validators passed. The endpoint returned 201 with `count: 16`, and all 16 rows came back `created: true`.
- **Re-price:** `prisma.deal.update` with the slug kept (`_ifs24i-reprice.cjs`).
