## Method

- **Stage 1:** `apps/api/scripts/ingest-ifs-proper.mjs` swept `/deals`, `/deals?page=2`, `/deals?page=3` and `/deals/superdeals`, waiting at least 2.5 s between requests. It resolved each base64 `?rto=` Buy Now link to the real store URL.
- **Dedup:** every product id was checked against the live DB by productId, affiliateUrl and slug (`_ifs24g-dedup.cjs`).
- **Amazon check:** done in the logged-in Playwright tab with `fetch('/dp/<ASIN>?th=1&psc=1')`:
  - Price: `#centerCol .a-price-whole`.
  - MRP: from the `#centerCol` text.
  - Stock: `#add-to-cart-button`.
  - Image: `#landingImage`.
- **MRP guard:** the MRP is dropped when price/MRP ≤ 0.2. Those rows carry no % or M.R.P. text.
- **Affiliate:** Amazon `tag=ashoksachdev-21` on `/dp/ASIN`. The IFS tag `dealhind-21` was stripped.
- **Copy:** rewritten from the product title and bullets only. No IFS text or images are used, and every image comes from `m.media-amazon.com`.
