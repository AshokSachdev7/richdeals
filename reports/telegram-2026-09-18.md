### Shortlink resolution — HEAD is useless

`curl -sIL` (HEAD) returns the **shortener URL itself** for `link.amazon/*` and
`rogerkart.com/r/*`. `curl -sL -o /dev/null -w '%{url_effective}'` (GET) resolves
`link.amazon` fine. `rogerkart.com/r/*` resolves for **neither** — it is a Next.js
client-side-JS Amazon shortener (`robots noindex,nofollow`); only a real browser tab
plus a ~4s wait lands on the `/dp/` URL. Use GET, and a browser tab for rogerkart.
