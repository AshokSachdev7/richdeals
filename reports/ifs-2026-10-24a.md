## Method

- **Stage 1:** swept `/deals?page=N` plus `/deals/superdeals`. Each base64 `?rto=` Buy Now was resolved to the real store URL, the source tag was stripped, and our affiliate link was applied per `ingest.config.json`.
  - Non-Amazon prices and stock were checked with `productLd()` (ld+json price within ±₹1, InStock).
- **Dedup:** every resolved `productId` was checked against the live DB. Dedup hits on LIVE rows were re-read on their product pages. The 4 product IDs were re-checked just before the push, with 0 hits.
- **Amazon check:** done in the logged-in Playwright tab with an in-page `fetch('/dp/<ASIN>?th=1&psc=1')`:
  - Price: `#centerCol .priceToPay`.
  - MRP: `.basisPrice`.
  - Stock: `#availability` plus `#add-to-cart-button`.
  - Image: `#landingImage[data-old-hires]`, or the landing image in `data-a-dynamic-image` when that is empty.
- **Flipkart check:** in-page fetch in a Playwright tab (curl gets a reCAPTCHA 403).
- **Price rule:** a deal is rejected if the page price is more than ₹1 above the card price. IFS card prices are often wrong, so every card was read on its product page.
- **MRP check:** if price/MRP ≤ 0.2, the MRP is nulled and the copy carries no % or M.R.P.
