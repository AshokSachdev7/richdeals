What the gap actually is

freekaamaal is not beating us on deals. Their estimated traffic sits in five clusters: telecom utility answers (recharge plans, USSD codes, balance checks, porting), OTT plan explainers, "best X in India" roundups, store coupon hubs, and — the part we cannot touch — liquor price pages, movie-piracy lists and adult web-series pages.

Their position profile is weak everywhere: of 10,000 gap keywords, 8 sit in positions 1–3 and 5,182 sit at 51 or worse. This is an opportunity map, not a fortress.

How to work this list

  • Tier 1 (est. 500+ visits, KD ≤ 35) — build these first. They are the intersection of what the tool scores as reachable and what it scores as worth reaching.
  • Telecom utility is the single cleanest gap. 145 new pages against ~26,800 estimated visits, and we currently have 2 of them. Recharge plans, USSD codes, data loan numbers, porting steps. Evergreen, no AdSense friction, and it feeds our recharge/store hubs.
  • OTT plans: we have zero of 39. Netflix / Prime / Hotstar / JioHotstar plan-and-price explainers. These need a refresh discipline — plan prices change, and a stale price page is worse than no page.
  • Store hubs are the natural asset and we only cover 5 of 104. Where the competitor URL is a bare store root (/redbus, /rapido), the answer is a store hub page, not a blog post — the owner directive already says surface every store hub even at zero live deals.
  • Best-X roundups: 304 new, but 164 are already covered. This is where duplicate-slug bloat starts. Check the "already covered" view before commissioning any of them.
  • Do not mass-produce. 1,090 pages at our 2–3/day blog cap is roughly a year of output. Scaled thin pages against a weak-authority domain is the failure mode, not the strategy — take tiers 1 and 2 (about 130 pages) as the real programme and re-cut the rest later.

On the "already covered" flag: it is a token-overlap match against our 296 post slugs, and it visibly over-matches — kfc offers today was scored a perfect match against our flipkart-offers-today-live-deals-2026, and best lip salve against best-smartphone-deals-india-2026. Read it as "check this before commissioning", never as confirmed coverage.
