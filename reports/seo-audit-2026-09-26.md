One defect was found:
- **8 static pages had no `og:image`, `og:url` or `og:type`.** The pages are `/about`, `/contact`, `/privacy`, `/terms`, `/disclosure`, `/forum`, `/submit` and `/compare`.
- **Cause:** each page sets its own `openGraph` object, typically just `{ title }`. Next.js merges metadata shallowly, so that object replaced the layout's `openGraph` completely and dropped the image, url and type.
- **Effect:** social shares and AI previews of these pages showed no card image.

## Fixes applied (commit 1091b34)
| Fix | Where | Prod verification |
|---|---|---|
| Each page's `openGraph` now sets `url: absUrl("/<page>")`, `type: "website"` and `images: [{ url: absUrl("/og.png"), width: 1200, height: 630 }]`. A root `opengraph-image` file was not used, because file-based metadata would override the product images on deal pages. | `app/{about,contact,privacy,terms,disclosure,forum,submit,compare}/page.tsx` | All 8 pages serve `og:image` = `https://richdeals.in/og.png`, their own `og:url` and `og:type` = `website`. |
