## Method

- **Stage 1:** `ingest-ifs-proper.mjs` swept `/deals` and `/deals/superdeals`. It resolved the base64 `?rto=` Buy Now ids.
- **Verification:** each price was read in the logged-in Amazon tab with a same-origin fetch:
  - price from `.a-price-whole` in `#corePriceDisplay`
  - stock from `#availability` and add-to-cart
  - MRP from `#centerCol` M.R.P.
  - image from the hiRes entry keyed by `landingAsinColor`
- **Dedup:** by productId against the live DB, and against `data/dd-rejected.json`.
- **≥80% rule:** when the MRP implies 80% off or more, `mrp` and `discountPct` are set to null and the title carries no "(N% Off)".
