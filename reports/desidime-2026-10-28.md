## Fix shipped this tick: Flipkart links now resolve without `?pid=`

Since this morning, DesiDime's Flipkart Buy Now links resolve to `/p/itm<hash>?affid=salescueli…`, with **no `pid`**. `affiliate()` needs a pid, so it nulled every one of them. Stage 1 then wrote the card id into `data/dd-dead.json`, which blocks that card **permanently**. On the first run 8 real Flipkart PDPs were lost this way: Lenovo Idea Tab, Samsung soundbar, ASUS TUF A15, Xiaomi 65", Samsung 32", Hisense AC, Haier washer and Galaxy S25 Ultra.

`ingest-desidime.mjs` now handles this. When a Flipkart `/p/itm` URL has no pid, it curls the PDP and takes the most frequent `"productId"` in the page. The Lenovo page had 278 hits for its own pid; the next most frequent related listing had 4. It then appends `?pid=` and retries `affiliate()`. I took the 8 card ids out of `dd-dead.json` and re-ran stage 1. All 8 resolved to pids whose ld+json titles match the cards. They were then rejected on real price drift and stamped into `dd-rejected.json` by the script.
