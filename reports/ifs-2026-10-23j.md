## Method

- **Stage 1:** `ingest-ifs-proper.mjs` swept `/deals?page=N` plus `/deals/superdeals`, decoded the base64 `?rto=` Buy Now links, and swapped in our affiliate tags per `ingest.config.json`.
- **Amazon check:** done in the logged-in Playwright tab with an in-page `fetch('/dp/<ASIN>?th=1&psc=1')`:
  - Price: `#centerCol .priceToPay`.
  - MRP: `.basisPrice`.
  - Stock: `#availability` plus `#add-to-cart-button`.
  - Image: `#landingImage[data-old-hires]`.
- **Price rule:** a deal is rejected if the page price is more than ₹1 above the IFS card price.
- **Dedup:** every `productId` was checked against the live DB. Every dedup hit was re-read on its product page.
- **MRP check:** if price/MRP ≤ 0.2, the MRP is nulled and the copy carries no % or M.R.P.
- **Copy:** spec claims that could not be confirmed on the product page were removed before posting.
