## Method

- **Sweep:** `/deals?page=N` plus `/deals/superdeals`, with at least 2.5 s between requests to indiafreestuff.in.
- **Buy Now:** each base64 `?rto=` deal id was resolved to the real store URL. The source's affiliate tag was stripped and ours applied per `ingest.config.json`.
- **Dedup:** every productId was checked against the DB, and the DB was also searched by `slug` and `affiliateUrl` to catch legacy rows with a null productId.
- **Amazon check:** done in the logged-in Playwright tab with an in-page `fetch('/dp/<ASIN>?th=1&psc=1')`, 2.6 s apart:
  - Price: `#centerCol .priceToPay`.
  - MRP: `.basisPrice`.
  - Stock: `#availability` plus `#add-to-cart-button`.
  - Image: `#landingImage[data-old-hires]`.
- **Flipkart check:** an in-tab fetch on flipkart.com of `/product/p/itme?pid=<PID>`, reading the ld+json `offers.price` and availability.
- **Rule:** a card is rejected if the page price is more than ₹1 from the card price, or the page has no buy box.
