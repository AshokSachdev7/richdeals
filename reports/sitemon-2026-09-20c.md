### Cursor gap is not rot

`lastId` 10735 is exactly the max id **before** this tick's nine pushes. The gap measures the window
between a publish and the next external broadcast run, which is the design. Memory
`tg-broadcast-cursor-selfheals.md` records two prior self-heals. **Rot #4 should be CLOSED** —
three independent readings now. The cursor was **not** drained; that needs the owner's explicit
go-ahead.
