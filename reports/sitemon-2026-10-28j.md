## Extra check: deal sitemap coverage

The INDEXNOW agent said the deal sitemap carries only the top-100 indexable deals. **That is true, and it is by design, not rot.**

| Sitemap | `<loc>` count |
|---|---|
| `/sitemap.xml` (index) | 3 children: pages, deals, blog |
| `/sitemaps/pages/sitemap.xml` | 63 |
| `/sitemaps/deals/sitemap.xml` | **100** (against 14,743 LIVE) |
| `/sitemaps/blog/sitemap.xml` | 424 |

- Deal #15279's slug is **not** in the deal sitemap. Its page returns 200 with `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, follow"/>`.
- The code in `apps/web/src/app/sitemaps/deals/sitemap.ts` reads `getTopDeals()`, which takes the slugs from `/deals/top-indexable` (the top 100 LIVE deals by clickCount). It then filters them with `dealIndexable(d, top)` from `lib/site.ts`, the same rule that sets the page's robots meta. A deal must be in the top 100, not EXPIRED, have a price and an image, have a discount of at least 20% or a description of at least 200 characters, and be at most 120 days old.
- This comes from **owner directive 2026-10-16**, in commit 216e250 ("index only top-100 deals"; every other deal page is noindex,follow and out of the sitemap, but stays live). Commit 6b8934b (10-19) split the sitemap into the per-type index.
- No change was made. Putting 14.6k noindex URLs into the sitemap would undo the owner's directive and send Google contradictory signals. No build or deploy was run.
- Doc drift: the CLAUDE.md lines "chunked sitemap" and "dynamic deal URLs come from the API automatically" are stale. They predate the 10-16 directive. Deal pings to IndexNow for non-top-100 slugs reach noindex pages, which is harmless but wasted.
