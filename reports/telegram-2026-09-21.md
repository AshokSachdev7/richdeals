### The coupon trap fired again, and the ratio test caught it

Dealzone and CoolzTricks both posted this dinner set "at ₹871". The PDP buybox is **₹1,244** and
the page carries a **30% off clip coupon**. `1244 × 0.70 = 870.8` → the channels' ₹871 is the
**post-coupon** price to the rupee. Published at the verified buybox ₹1,244 with the coupon
recorded in `couponNote`, not as the price. This is the fifth confirmation of
"never treat a channel's 'at ₹X' as a buybox price".

calls that file binary.)
