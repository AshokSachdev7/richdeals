### Rot #4 is materially closed — the cursor moved on its own

The 09-19g baseline recorded the broadcast cursor stuck at **10,482** against a DB max of 10,687 —
**205 deals behind**, and the standing note said only the external tg-broadcast cron could close it.

It is now at **10,719**. That cron **is running**, it processed the whole 205-row backlog plus
everything published today up to and including the Shopsy deal, and the remaining drift of 15 is
precisely the DEAL-INGEST batch pushed minutes before this tick. It will clear on the next
broadcast run.

**Correction to the standing rot list:** #4 should be downgraded from "growing, unattended" to
"healthy, lags by one batch". Every tick report today — including the two written an hour ago —
said "grew by N more; only the external cron closes it". The cron was closing it the whole time;
nobody had re-read the cursor file since the 09-19 baseline. **This is the CEO-audit rule catching
the auditor**, which is the failure mode worth recording: a carried-forward finding is not
evidence, and I repeated one three times today without re-measuring it.
