## Method

- **Stage 1:** `ingest-ifs-proper.mjs` swept `/deals?page=N` and `/deals/superdeals`, resolved the base64 `?rto=` Buy Now links to the real store URLs, and dropped event pages, sale hubs and nulled Flipkart `/indiafreestuff/p/` cards.
- **Amazon:** every ASIN was read in the logged-in Playwright tab with an in-page `fetch('/dp/…?th=1&psc=1')`:
  - Price: `#centerCol .priceToPay .a-price-whole`.
  - MRP: `.basisPrice`.
  - Stock: `#availability` plus `#add-to-cart-button`.
  - Image: `data-old-hires`. B0FFBL74ZV had none, so its image came from the `hiRes` regex.
  - The IFS card price was never trusted. KOTTY B0F6VBJF16 went in at the page's ₹300, not the card's ₹305.
- **Dedup:** every resolved `productId` was checked against the live DB. Rows that already existed were re-read on the product page instead of being skipped.
- **MRP ratio check:** where price/MRP ≤ 0.2 the MRP is nulled, so no % or M.R.P. text is shown.
