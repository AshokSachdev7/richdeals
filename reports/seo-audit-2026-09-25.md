## Meta sweep
- All 25 URLs return 200.
- Every URL has exactly one h1 and a self-referencing canonical.
- EXPIRED deals carry `noindex, follow`, which is correct.

Two defects were found:
1. **Deal names truncated at the first `|` or ` - `.** `dealSeoTitle()` and `dealProductName()` in `apps/web/src/lib/site.ts` used `split("|")[0].split(/\s[–-]\s/)[0]`. That reduced these titles to their prefix:
   - "Myntra | Upto 60% Off…" became **"Myntra"**.
   - "Amazon Brand - Symbol Girls…" became **"Amazon Brand"**.
   - "GO DESi - Mysore Pak" became **"GO DESi"**.
   - "Mattress | 10 Years Warranty…" became **"Mattress"**.

   The damage hit the `<title>`, `og:title`, anchors and the Product schema name. It affected 150 LIVE deals plus EXPIRED ones.
2. **The legal pages (`/contact`, `/disclosure`, `/privacy`, `/terms`) had no `openGraph` block.**
   - Their `og:title` fell back to the homepage default ("RichDeals — Best Deals, Coupons & Freebies in India").
   - Their descriptions were short: 133, 84, 106 and 75 characters.

## Not fixed and why
- **`/offers` sends `s-maxage=31536000`.** This is the owner's caching decision and is carried over.
- **`https://www.richdeals.in/` returns 000.** It needs a DNS record, which counts as touching the owner's account and credentials.
- **No h2 on `/stores`, `/categories`, `/submit` or `/category/shopping-site/amazon`.** Carried over. It is a low-value template change.
- **`/forum` description is 144 characters and the `/about` title is 20 characters.** Carried over. These are brand-style pages.
- **The Myntra EXPIRED deal title keeps a trailing "." and its meta description quotes the raw "Myntra |" title.** This is cosmetic, and the page is noindex.

## Retracted findings
- **`noalt=2` on every page.** These are the decorative logo SVGs with `alt=""` next to the brand text. That is correct accessibility markup, not a defect.
