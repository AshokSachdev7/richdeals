## Sitemap composition — the 1,039-URL question

`curl -s https://richdeals.in/sitemap.xml | grep -c '<loc>'` → **9577**, against 10,278 LIVE
deals. Bucketed by first path segment:

| Bucket | URLs |
|---|---|
| Deal slugs (root level) | 9239 |
| `/blog` + posts | 301 |
| `/category/*` | 12 |
| `/stores/*` | 8 |
| `/compare/*` | 5 |
| Root `/` | 1 |
| Other static hubs | 11 |
| **Total** | **9577** |

Three gaps, all three explained by `apps/web/src/app/sitemap.ts` — deliberate filters with
their reasons written into the file:

**Deals — 10,278 LIVE vs 9,239 submitted.** `allDeals.filter(dealIndexable)`, the same
helper the deal page's own robots rule uses, so the sitemap cannot drift from page-level
noindex. Recomputed `dealIndexable` over every LIVE row:

```
LIVE 10278  indexable 9239
excluded: noPrice 0 · noImage 0 · notValuable 1039 · olderThan120d 0
```

**9239 computed = 9239 in the sitemap, exact.** Every excluded row fails the "valuable"
test: under 20% discount *and* under a 200-char description. That is thin-content
suppression working as designed, not deals going missing. Nothing is older than 120 days,
so the age arm of the predicate is currently inert.

**Posts — 315 published vs 301 submitted.** `!(p.slug in POST_REDIRECTS)`: 14 slugs folded
into keepers by `next.config` redirects answer 308. Matches the consolidation commits
`1158088` and `050e70a`. Submitting a URL we redirect away from is the defect; the filter
prevents it.

**Stores — 254 vs 8.** `(s.liveDeals ?? 1) > 0 || hasStoreSeo(s.slug)`. The 245 imported
directory hubs with neither stay LIVE and linked from `/stores` per the 2026-08-08 owner
directive (never 404 them), but they are not submitted — the file's own comment says why:
empty near-duplicate pages are what a "crawled, not indexed" pile is made of.

Recorded as **explained, not rot.** No new rot item opened.
