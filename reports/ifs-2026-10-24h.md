## Method

- **Sweep:** `/deals?page=N` and `/deals/superdeals`, with at least 2.5 s between IFS requests. Each Buy Now `?rto=` is a base64 deal id; it was resolved to the real merchant URL, the IFS tag was stripped, and our affiliate link was applied per `ingest.config.json`:
  - Amazon: `tag=ashoksachdev-21`
  - Flipkart: `/p/itm…?pid=…&affid=djhackraj`
  - Myntra: InRDeals
  - every other store: Cuelinks
- **Dedup:** every productId was checked against the live DB with Prisma.
- **Amazon check:** in-tab `fetch('/dp/<ASIN>?th=1&psc=1')`:
  - price from `#corePriceDisplay_desktop_feature_div .priceToPay .a-price-whole`
  - MRP from `.basisPrice .a-offscreen`
  - stock from `#add-to-cart-button`
- **Flipkart check:** in-tab `fetch('/x/p/x?pid=PID')` in a logged-in tab, reading the ld+json `offers.price`. The correct itm path is the `"url"` value that carries `pid=<PID>`; the first `/p/itm` on the page is usually a recommended product.
- **Myntra check:** curl with a browser UA. Price and availability come from the ld+json `Product.offers`; MRP from `"mrp":N`.
- **MRP guard:** `(!m||m<=p||m>=p*100||p/m<=0.2) → null`. When it fires, the title and description carry no % or M.R.P. text.
- **Copy:** original, written only from product-page facts. Images come from the marketplace CDNs (`m.media-amazon.com`, `rukmini1.flixcart.com`, Myntra), never from IFS.
